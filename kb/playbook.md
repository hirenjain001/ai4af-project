# Merchandising playbook

## Open-to-buy policy

Draft actions must remain within open-to-buy. A recommendation that exceeds
the available open-to-buy amount must be escalated to a planner.

## Markdown policy

Non-core styles may be drafted for markdown up to 20 percent off. Deeper
markdowns require human approval and must never be auto-scheduled.

## Vendor lead time

When a vendor cutoff is five days away or less and no planner has decided, the
style must be escalated. The agent may draft a recommendation but may not
submit or cancel a vendor purchase order.

## Size curve

Extended-size ranges must be evaluated separately from core-size ranges. A
high-buzz signal alone is not sufficient evidence for a chase.
